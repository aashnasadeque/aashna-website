04 / RESEARCH + AI2026

AI Coding Agent Analysis

Research into how familiarity affects acceptance of AI generated pull requests.

PYTHON · REGRESSION · RESEARCH

Our study examined 33,596 AI generated pull requests across 2,807 GitHub repositories. I developed and debugged analysis scripts, corrected preprocessing, tested sensitivity across activity thresholds, and helped interpret logistic regression models. Prior merge success was associated with roughly four times greater odds of a subsequent merge.
